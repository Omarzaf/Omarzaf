# Muhammad Umar Zafar

I am an AI policy researcher, geopolitical analyst, and nonprofit founder
building source-auditable research tools and public-interest technology.

## Selected work

1. **[PakTechPolicy](https://github.com/Omarzaf/PakTechPolicy)** — A source-auditable public index of Pakistan technology policy, indicators, and official evidence.
2. **[Reasoning Across Borders](https://github.com/Omarzaf/testing-ai-geolocation-hypothesis)** — A public benchmark and research design for examining geographic differences in consumer AI behavior.
3. **[SignBridge Overlay](https://github.com/Omarzaf/signbridge-overlay)** — A human-reviewed sign-language overlay protocol and local-first runtime for online and offline video.
4. **[Gen Z Protest Atlas](https://github.com/Omarzaf/meowmeow-atlas)** — A source-backed atlas of youth-led protest, digital resistance, international rights, and OSINT methods.
5. **[Rock to Rack](https://github.com/Omarzaf/rock-to-rack)** — An educational browser game about the semiconductor supply chain from raw materials to computing infrastructure.
6. **[RDL](https://github.com/Omarzaf/RDL)** — A source-auditable map of lobbying relationships and epistemic communities in Washington, D.C.
